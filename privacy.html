Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The short version

Image Downloader does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. The extension runs entirely inside your browser. There is no server, no account, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising code.

What the extension does

When you click the extension icon on a web page:

  1. It injects a scanner script into that one page to find images — img elements, srcset candidates, source tags, CSS background-image declarations, and links pointing at image files.
  2. It shows what it found as a thumbnail grid in the popup.
  3. If you select images and press Download, it hands those URLs to Chrome's own download manager, which saves the files to your computer.

That is the whole flow. The extension does not read page text, form values, cookies, passwords, or any other page content.

Data stored on your device

The extension uses chrome.storage.local to remember, on your machine only:

  • Your toggle settings — auto-scroll, hide tiny icons, ALT text as filename, convert WebP/AVIF to JPG
  • Your filter values — format, minimum width, minimum height
  • Local counters, such as how many times you ran a scan, shown to nobody but you

This data never leaves your device, is never sent anywhere, and is not tied to any identifier. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no signup, no login
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting
  • No advertising, no affiliate links, no sponsored content
  • No third-party SDKs or trackers of any kind
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of data — we hold none to sell
  • No reading of any page unless you click the extension icon on it

Permissions, and why each one exists

PermissionWhy it is needed
activeTabTo scan the page you opened the extension on. Access is scoped to that tab and granted only when you click the icon.
scriptingTo inject the image-scanner script into that tab.
downloadsTo save the images you selected through Chrome's download manager.
storageTo remember your filter and toggle preferences locally.
optional host permissionsNot granted at install. Chrome asks for access to sites only if you turn on WebP/AVIF to JPG conversion, which has to fetch the image from the server that hosts it. Turn the option off or remove the access in chrome://extensions and the extension goes back to reading only the tab you click it on.

Image data

Image URLs and dimensions found on a page live in memory while the popup is open and are discarded when it closes. They are never logged, transmitted, or stored.

Image conversion

If you enable WebP/AVIF to JPG conversion, the extension fetches the image bytes and re-encodes them locally, inside your browser, using an offscreen canvas. Nothing is uploaded to any service for conversion.

Children

The extension collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear at this same URL with an updated date above.
